Salary calculator Australia guide

A salary calculator helps show what an Australian pay figure may look like after tax. Instead of focusing only on headline salary, it becomes easier to understand estimated net income, income tax, Medicare levy and employer super in one place.

This is especially useful when comparing job offers, deciding whether a package looks competitive, or planning household cash flow. A higher salary can look attractive at first glance, but the real comparison often comes down to after-tax pay and the value of employer super.

This Australia salary calculator is designed for people who want a fast, clear estimate. It shows annual, monthly, fortnightly and weekly take-home pay, and it can also handle package figures that already include super. For first-time users, tooltips explain common terms such as Medicare levy, base salary and salary package.

The calculator is best used for straightforward salary estimates. It does not currently model every adjustment, such as HELP repayments, salary sacrifice arrangements, deductions, Medicare levy surcharge, private health insurance outcomes or all tax offsets.

Frequently asked questions

What does this salary calculator show?

It estimates take-home pay, income tax, Medicare levy and employer super based on the selected salary and settings.

Can it show weekly and fortnightly pay?

Yes. It shows annual, monthly, fortnightly and weekly take-home pay estimates.

What happens if salary includes super?

When that option is enabled, the entered figure is treated as a total package. The calculator backs out employer super to estimate base salary and net pay.

Is this calculator suitable for foreign residents?

Yes. It includes a foreign resident setting so tax can be estimated using foreign resident rates in this version.

Important note This calculator provides an estimate only. It does not include every adjustment, deduction, repayment or offset that may affect final tax outcomes.
